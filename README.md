## 🔭 Flagship Work & Research Systems

### 🛡️ AI Agent Security & Governance
* **[Orvyen Agentic](https://github.com/orvyen/orvyen-agentic)** &bull; *Static Security & Compliance Scanner for AI Agents*  
  Pre-deployment SAST security engine purpose-built for modern AI agent architectures (**LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, and Model Context Protocol - MCP**).
  * Sub-10ms AST parsing with zero mandatory runtime dependencies.
  * Auto-generates **Agent Bill of Materials (AIBOM)** with tool privilege tiers (`READ`, `WRITE`, `EXECUTE`, `ADMIN`).
  * Direct regulatory scorecards for **Singapore IMDA Model AI Governance Framework (v1.5)** and **OWASP LLM Top 10 (2025)**.

### 🏥 Medical AI, Forensics & Privacy
* **[BioVault](https://github.com/rizzshi/biovault)** &bull; *Privacy-Preserving Cyber Threat Detection for IoMT*  
  Decentralized Federated Learning (FL) combined with Differential Privacy Stochastic Gradient Descent (DP-SGD) for collaborative threat intelligence across distributed healthcare devices.
* **[GhostScan](https://github.com/rizzshi/ghostscan)** &bull; *Adversarial Forensics & Diagnostics for Medical Vision*  
  Dual-stream forensic framework for adversarial robustness and diagnostic reliability in clinical imaging architectures.
* **[MedTwin-Hunter](https://github.com/rizzshi/medtwin-hunter)** &bull; *Medical Digital Twin Diagnostics & Anomaly Hunting*  
  Diagnostic modeling and anomaly detection for patient digital twins and physiological telemetry.
